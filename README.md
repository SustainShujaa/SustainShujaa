# Jonathan R. Santana

### AI Systems & Operational Intelligence Architect  
Program & Technology Leader

I design coordination systems that bring together AI, deterministic software, data, evidence, tools, and human authority around consequential operational work.

My work sits at the intersection of program leadership, government acquisition, operational improvement, and technology product development. I start with the real operating problem: what decision needs to be made, what evidence supports it, who has authority to act, and how the result can be checked later. AI can help people investigate, organize, and prepare decisions. It should not quietly acquire authority or replace accountable judgment.

I am the founder of [Excelsior Consulting Services](https://excelsiorcs.com), where I lead work in program and project management, acquisition support, and technology-enabled operational solutions. My background includes more than two decades supporting complex government and commercial programs.

## Working principles

- Treat authority as explicit.
- Use deterministic controls where they are appropriate.
- Preserve uncertainty instead of hiding it.
- Make consequential actions reviewable and traceable.
- Build useful systems around real operational constraints.

The repositories here are independent public references and case-study material. They are designed to demonstrate capability without exposing client information, private source code, or proprietary implementation details.
